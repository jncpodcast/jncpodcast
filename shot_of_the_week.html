Featured Recipe

The Paper Plane

The vibe: Bright, bittersweet, and beautifully balanced—bourbon brings the warmth while Aperol, Amaro Nonino, and fresh lemon keep this modern classic ready for takeoff.

Ingredients
  • Bourbon¾ oz
  • Aperol¾ oz
  • Amaro Nonino¾ oz
  • Fresh lemon juice¾ oz
Method

Add all ingredients to a shaker with ice. Shake until well chilled, then strain into a coupe glass. Garnish with a small paper plane pick if desired.

“Equal parts, perfectly balanced, and cleared for takeoff.”

The Paper Plane brings bourbon, Aperol, Amaro Nonino, and fresh lemon juice together in equal measure for a cocktail that lands between rich, citrusy, herbal, and pleasantly bitter.

Created by bartender Sam Ross, this modern classic is simple to remember, fast to shake, and exactly the kind of smart-but-unfussy drink that belongs behind the JNC bar.

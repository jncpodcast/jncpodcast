Featured Recipe

The Bicicletta

The vibe: Crisp, sparkling, and pleasantly bittersweet—dry white wine keeps it light while Campari and club soda give this Italian-style aperitivo its vivid color and refreshing snap.

Ingredients
  • Dry white wine3 oz
  • Campari2 oz
  • Chilled club sodaTo top
  • Orange wheels2
Method

Pour the white wine and Campari into a wine glass or double rocks glass. Fill the glass about three-quarters full with ice, top with chilled club soda, and stir gently. Garnish with two orange wheels.

“Bittersweet, bubbly, and built for the ride.”

The Bicicletta combines dry white wine and Campari, then stretches everything with chilled club soda for a bright red aperitivo that stays crisp, refreshing, and easy to sip.

Served over plenty of ice with two orange wheels, it brings bold citrus and herbal bitterness without weighing down the evening—the perfect kind of drink for a long, relaxed stop at the JNC bar.
